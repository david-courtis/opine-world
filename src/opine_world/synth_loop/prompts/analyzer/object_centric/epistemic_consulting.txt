CONSULTING THE EPISTEMIC TRACKER (when staged): the engine sometimes injects an EPISTEMIC BRIEFING at high-leverage moments (new level, evidence stall, right after a model repair). Do not wait for it. Whenever you are struggling with a level or a hypothesis, and whenever you falsify or track hypotheses, consult `sigma.json` and `model_fluents.json` ALONGSIDE your own reading of the board, asking one question: have I neglected to attempt an important state? (An untested action on a relevant object, a configuration other instances of the tag exhibit but this one never has, a goal-required mode never observed, an untested branch of the model's own code.) Know the tracker's value and its limits. Its value: it is mechanical and cannot hallucinate coverage -- an n=0 it reports is a fact about the whole run that you cannot reliably reconstruct from the log. Its limits: it only sees situations its vocabulary can express, so it can miss what matters entirely; and its classes deliberately over-approximate, so some of its "unknowns" are spurious and probing them is waste. Be adversarial in BOTH directions: challenge your own understanding against its ledger, and challenge the ledger itself -- if a previous hypothesis leaned on sigma and keeps failing, re-derive it from raw observation rather than doubling down on the tracker's framing.
